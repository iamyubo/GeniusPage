This is my ability tutorial about how to use Raze from Valorant effectively!

Boom Bot

Raze is a duelist with a damage oriented kit that specializes in clearing tight spaces. The first ability, boom bot, will autotrack the enemy and distract them. It deals 80 damage, making the enemy have to choose between you or the bot. It's also extremely useful to check places where you think the enemy may be and may help you net a free kill.

Blast Pack

Her second ability, the blast pack / satchel, is extremely versatile. It can be used to help you cover great distances, peek corners, and throw off the enemy's aim. To satchel jump, throw a blast pack and jump over it and activate the ability again when you are halfway to the ground to explode it, propelling you based on your position relative to the satchel. By throwing a satchel on the wall, you can peek safely by exploding it as you are walking the direction you want to go. By throwing a satchel near the enemy and exploding it, the enemy gets propelled and their aim gets messed up, leading to a free kill.

Paint Shells

Her third ability, paint shells, is used as area denial. It can be thrown to places where you suspect the enemy is to either push them back, make them move forward, or give you a free kill. It's extremely usedful in tight corners so the enemy won't be able to run. it restocks every two kills, and is one of the best abilities in her kit.

Showstopper

Her ult, showstopper, fires an rocket that does massive damage on contact with anything. It can be used to rocket jump or can be paired with the satchels to help you deal massive aoe damage from the sky. Try to only pop this when you know there are multiple enemies bundled together to get a lot of kills. It basically guarantees a free win in a trade.
